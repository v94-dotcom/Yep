Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

The short version: Yep. collects nothing. Your data stays on your phone. We don't have servers. We can't see your data even if we wanted to.

What data does Yep. collect?

None. Yep. does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All app data — including your items, confirmation timestamps, and photos — is stored locally on your device only.

Photos

When you use the optional photo proof feature, photos are saved to your device's local app storage. These photos are:

  • Never uploaded to any server or cloud service
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Never accessible to anyone other than you
  • Automatically deleted after 24 hours

Photos are stored in the app's private directory and are not visible in your device's gallery or photo library.

Camera permission

Yep. requests camera access only if you choose to use the photo proof feature. The camera is used solely to capture confirmation photos. You can use Yep. fully without granting camera permission — the photo feature is always optional.

Notification permission

If you choose to enable daily reminders, Yep. will request notification permission. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

Analytics and tracking

Yep. does not include any analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, advertising frameworks, or third-party services of any kind. We do not track how you use the app, how often you open it, or what items you create.

Advertising

Yep. contains no advertisements and no advertising SDKs.

Third-party services

Yep. does not integrate with any third-party services. The app operates entirely offline and does not make any network requests.

User accounts

Yep. does not require or support user accounts. There is no sign-up, login, email collection, or any form of registration.

Data storage and deletion

All data is stored locally using your device's standard app storage. You can delete all Yep. data at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's data in your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

Since no data exists on any server, uninstalling the app permanently and completely removes all data associated with Yep.

Children's privacy

Yep. does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all, it is compliant with COPPA and similar regulations by design.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this privacy policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above. Since Yep. collects no data, any future changes would only apply if the app's functionality fundamentally changed.
